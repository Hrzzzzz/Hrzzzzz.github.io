Working Papers

Incorporating Return Prediction in High-Dimensional Mean-Variance Portfolio Optimization

Ruizhao Huang, Yingying Li, Xinghua Zheng

Major Revision at Management Science

SSRN ↗

Abstract: We develop a framework that incorporates return prediction to achieve unconditional mean-variance efficiency in high dimensions. Our method, termed MAXSER-PR, builds upon the seminal “MAXSER” estimator of Ao, Li, and Zheng (2019) by integrating conditional information into the optimization, thereby providing a tractable bridge between conditional return prediction and unconditional mean-variance efficiency. This integration is complicated by the fact that conditional efficiency does not imply unconditional efficiency, by the lack of historical data under conditional distributions, and by high dimensionality. We address these challenges by deriving a regression representation of a conditional optimization whose solution achieves unconditional mean-variance efficiency; constructing synthetic returns; and utilizing random matrix theory and elastic net regression to obtain a feasible and stable solution. Under an elliptical model for the unpredictable return component that allows for heteroskedasticity, heavy-tailedness and serial dependence, and an elastic net regularity condition, our method asymptotically attains unconditional mean-variance efficiency. We demonstrate that our method delivers superior performance through comprehensive empirical studies across multiple prediction models and high-dimensional settings.
